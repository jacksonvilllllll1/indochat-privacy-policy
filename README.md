# IndoChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Welcome to IndoChat: Chat with friends and family!

This Privacy Policy explains how IndoChat collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our app and services.

IndoChat is an international messaging platform with an Indonesian-inspired design and is intended for teens and older users.

## 1. Age Requirement

IndoChat is intended for users who meet the minimum age requirement shown during registration and in the app's store listing.

Users who do not meet the minimum age requirement must not create or use an IndoChat account.

If we learn that an account belongs to someone below the required minimum age, we may restrict or delete that account.

Parents or guardians who believe a minor has created an account in violation of our age requirement can contact us using the support information provided in the app.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use IndoChat, we may collect:

### Account information

- Email address
- Password information needed for authentication
- Username
- Display name
- Profile picture
- Country
- Optional phone number
- Account creation date
- Account preferences

Passwords should be securely processed and should never be stored in plain text.

### Messages and content

If you use IndoChat's messaging features, we may process:

- Messages you send
- Group messages
- Message reactions
- Images or files you choose to send
- Voice messages, if supported
- Other content you voluntarily submit

### Device and technical information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as:

- Device type
- Operating system
- App version
- IP address
- Crash and diagnostic information
- Security and authentication information

We use this information to maintain, troubleshoot, and protect IndoChat.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Create and maintain your account
- Provide messaging and social features
- Deliver notifications
- Provide voice or video calling where supported
- Maintain your friend and group lists
- Authenticate users
- Prevent spam, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
- Respond to support requests
- Improve the app
- Fix bugs and technical problems
- Maintain security
- Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

We do not use private messages to create advertising profiles.

## 4. Messages and Private Content

Your messages are intended for the people or groups you choose to communicate with.

However, messages may be processed or stored by the systems required to provide the messaging service.

IndoChat may access or review content when reasonably necessary to:

- Respond to a support request
- Investigate abuse or violations of our rules
- Protect users or the service
- Prevent illegal activity
- Comply with a valid legal obligation

We do not sell private messages to advertisers.

## 5. Friends and Profiles

Information that you choose to make visible through your profile may be visible to other IndoChat users.

Depending on your privacy settings, this may include:

- Username
- Display name
- Profile picture
- Country
- Status/about information
- Online status

You should avoid publicly sharing sensitive personal information such as your home address, passwords, financial information, or private contact details.

## 6. Location Information

IndoChat does not need your precise GPS location to provide ordinary messaging features.

If a future feature requires location information, IndoChat will explain why it is needed and request permission where required.

## 7. Contacts

If IndoChat offers contact discovery or contact synchronization, the app will request permission before accessing contacts.

Contact information will only be used for the purposes described when the feature is enabled.

You can disable contact access through your device settings.

## 8. Third-Party Sign-In

IndoChat may support sign-in services such as:

- Google Sign-In
- Apple Sign-In

When you use a third-party sign-in service, that provider may process information according to its own privacy policy.

IndoChat only receives the information made available through the authentication process.

## 9. Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party providers to operate parts of IndoChat, such as:

- Authentication
- Database hosting
- Cloud storage
- Push notifications
- Analytics and diagnostics
- Voice/video communication
- Security services

These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle it according to applicable agreements and laws.

## 10. Advertising

IndoChat does not sell personal information to advertisers.

If advertising is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how advertising and related data processing work before the feature is introduced.

## 11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee that information will always be completely secure.

Users should protect their passwords and never share them with other people.

## 12. Blocking and Reporting

IndoChat provides tools that may allow users to:

- Block other users
- Report accounts
- Report messages or content
- Manage unwanted contact

Reports may be reviewed by IndoChat or its service providers when necessary to enforce our rules and protect users.

## 13. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our policies.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or logs before being automatically removed.

## 14. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your IndoChat account through the account settings or available support channels.

Account deletion may permanently remove information associated with your account, including your profile and conversations, subject to technical limitations and legal requirements.

Some information may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate purposes.

## 15. International Users

IndoChat is intended for users in multiple countries.

Your information may therefore be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

## 16. Children's Privacy

IndoChat is not intended for children below its stated minimum age requirement.

We do not knowingly provide accounts to users who do not meet that requirement.

If we discover that a user does not meet the minimum age requirement, we may take steps to remove or restrict the account and associated information as required by applicable law.

## 17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when IndoChat's features, technology, or legal requirements change.

When significant changes are made, we may provide notice through the app or another appropriate method.

The "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy will show when the policy was most recently changed.

## 18. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy practices, or your personal information, contact IndoChat through the official support contact provided inside the app.
